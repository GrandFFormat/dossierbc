✋ What is “challenging”?

One click on “Ask for an explanation” = a citizen request for the bill's sponsor to explain it in plain language, and for us to keep an eye on it together.

An account (email) is required — one request per person, no anonymous requests.

In British Columbia, these requests lead to nothing official: the Assembly has no online petitions, unlike Quebec or the House of Commons. For now, it is a simple way to see which laws trouble people. Some day, an elected member may find a use for it.
