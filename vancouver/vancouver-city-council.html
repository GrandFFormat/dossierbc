Vancouver City Council

The members Votes against, by member

Vancouver City Council has eleven members: the Mayor and ten Councillors, all elected by the whole city for four years. There are no wards and no districts — every member answers to every voter. The members below are the people who cast votes in the period; their votes come from the City’s electronic voting record, not from a register of attendance. The record publishes no party affiliation and no contact information, so none is shown. Their decisions are on the Decisions page.
