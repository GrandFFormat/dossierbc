How to read this page

  • The amount is the bid amount of the winning submission, as the City publishes it. Later changes to the contract are not in this data: the final cost may differ.
  • The bid type is the City’s procurement route: Request for Proposal, Invitation to Tender, Request for Quotation, Request for Application (pre-qualification), Expression of Interest.
  • Who bid: the City publishes every submission on each call, with “awarded: Yes” for the winner and “No” for the others. Opening a contract shows them all, with the gap to the winning amount.
  • What is not here: contracts awarded without a public call, contract amendments, and the link between a contract and a Council decision. The City does not publish those in this data set, so this page does not show them.
  • The data lags. The City refreshes this data set a few times a year; the last award it contains is shown at the bottom of the page. An empty recent period means the data has not been refreshed, not that nothing was awarded.
