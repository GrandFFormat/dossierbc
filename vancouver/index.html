What this site covers

Since 2016, Vancouver City Council votes electronically, and the City publishes every vote of every member as open data: Council and Special Council meetings, Public Hearings, the two standing committees, Courts of Revision and the Auditor General Committee. That record is what you see here — 83,824 individual votes since April 2016 in the City’s data set, of which this preview shows two months.

Each card is one motion put to a vote: the item as the agenda names it, the meeting, the result, and who voted which way. When a member declared a conflict of interest and left the room, the card says so. The City publishes no text of its decisions in this data — only the agenda line — so there are no plain-language summaries here, and nothing is written in their place.

The vocabulary (rezoning, CD-1, standing committee, referral) is explained in the glossary, with the number of times each term appears in the period. The Sources page says where every number comes from and what a machine can miss.
