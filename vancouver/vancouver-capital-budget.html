How to read this page

  • The capital budget is not the whole City budget. It covers investments — what gets built or bought — and not day-to-day operations (salaries, police, fire, garbage collection), which are in the operating budget.
  • “2026 expenditure” is what the City plans to actually spend in 2026 on each project. “Open budget” is the total approved for the project across years, part of which may already be spent. A project can show a large open budget and a small 2026 expenditure: the work is spread over several years.
  • The forecasts for 2027 to 2030 are the City’s planning figures at the time of the 2026 budget. They change every year.
  • What is not here: the operating budget and the property tax rates. The City does not publish them as open data; they are in its budget documents. This page does not estimate your tax bill, because it would have to invent the rate.
