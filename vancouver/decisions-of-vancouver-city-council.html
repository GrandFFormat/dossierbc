Decisions and recorded votes of Vancouver City Council

Here are the decisions of Vancouver City Council: each card is a motion put to a vote — a staff recommendation, a by-law, an amendment or a member’s motion — as the City’s electronic voting record names it, grouped by meeting. Open a card to see who voted for, who voted against, who abstained, who was absent and who declared a conflict. The members are on the City Council page.

What a card contains. The City publishes the agenda line of each item and every member’s vote — not the text of the motion, not the minutes. So there is no plain-language summary here, and nothing is written in its place. The link on each card leads to the City’s own record of that meeting’s votes; the official minutes are published separately by the City Clerk.
