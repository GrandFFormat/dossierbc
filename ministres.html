Composition of the Assembly

How to read vote attendance Full definition → Glossary

88%: out of 100 recorded divisions held by the House since the legislature opened, the person voted in 88 — yea or nay, whichever way.

  • Only divisions of the House count. Committees sit in Section A, B or C with about a dozen members: their divisions say nothing about anyone else’s attendance, and are left out. Someone elected at a by-election is counted from the day of their election, not before.
  • —: the Speaker of the Assembly*. To stay neutral, the Speaker only votes to break a tie: a low rate would not mean an absence.
  • n/a: no recorded vote for this person.
  • This is not an official measure: the Assembly does not publish attendance, and a missed vote does not tell why.

* The Assembly's President is not the same as the Premier: the President runs the debates and stays neutral, while the Premier heads the government and votes like the other members. See the Assembly's presidency in the Glossary →

** Updated every week: the site rereads the recorded divisions written in the Assembly's Journals and recalculates each person's rate, then the median, the colours and the numbers above. A vote shows up here once its Journal is published.
